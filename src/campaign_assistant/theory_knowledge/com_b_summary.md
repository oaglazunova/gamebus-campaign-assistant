# COM-B summary

COM-B explains behavior as arising from interaction between:

- Capability: the person's psychological and physical ability to perform the behavior.
- Opportunity: external physical and social conditions that enable or constrain the behavior.
- Motivation: reflective and automatic processes that energize and direct behavior.
- Behavior: the target action.

Campaign review questions inspired by COM-B:

- Capability: Does the campaign explain what to do? Does it build skills gradually? Are tasks feasible?
- Opportunity: Does the campaign account for context, environment, social support, timing, and access?
- Motivation: Does the campaign use feedback, rewards, progress, reminders, identity, or confidence-building?
- Behavior specificity: Are target behaviors concrete enough to act on and measure?

COM-B feedback should be framed as advisory theory-oriented reflection, not formal validation.
