# Transtheoretical Model summary

The Transtheoretical Model (TTM) describes behavior change as movement through stages, often including:

- Precontemplation: not yet intending to change.
- Contemplation: considering change.
- Preparation: intending or preparing to act.
- Action: actively changing behavior.
- Maintenance: sustaining change and preventing relapse.
- Relapse or recycling: returning to an earlier stage can occur.

Campaign review questions inspired by TTM:

- Does the campaign distinguish users who are not ready, preparing, acting, or maintaining?
- Does task difficulty increase gradually?
- Are relapse or at-risk paths handled supportively?
- Are feedback and goals appropriate to the user's stage?
- Does the campaign avoid assuming that all users are equally ready to change?

TTM feedback should not assume one fixed campaign structure unless the campaign explicitly defines that structure.
